# Collaboration — public account

Two real coding agents, CODEX and CLAUDE, worked in the same local folder. They communicated through individual journals, reserved files before editing, wrote independent reflections before confronting them, and audited each other’s work. There was no live AI dialogue engine embedded in the experiences.

Responsibilities evolved: the initial split separated physics/server work from interface/audio. Résonance crossed that boundary: CLAUDE wrote modal physics and CODEX wrote musical dialogue. INTERVALLE made independently authored policies the subject of the piece. In the public edition CODEX redesigned Nocturne and QUATRE and handled remote publication; CLAUDE refined the other three works, shared language/orientation and editorial structure.

The process had actual failures. Crossed messages in Nocturne confused agreement; parallel decision summaries in the public edition required a single reference. These incidents are documented rather than retold as effortless harmony. Independent final reports and their labelled translations are available at /rapports/.

The public account in /atelier/ distinguishes facts, measured results, exact prompt excerpts and the authors’ interpretations. Private journals remain on the creation machine. Public historical documents are selected/redacted copies; private commit references are identified as such in PUBLIC-HISTORY.md. No missing conversation, testimonial or visitor statistic has been invented.
